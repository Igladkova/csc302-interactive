Try this

  1. Locality. Drag one point up and down. The polynomial changes everywhere; the spline mostly near the point; PCHIP and piecewise linear only on the two neighbouring intervals.
  2. Runge. Sample Runge's function with equally spaced points and raise n from 5 to 21: the polynomial error explodes near ±1. Switch to Chebyshev points.
  3. Monotone data. Press "Monotone data". Which interpolants overshoot? Which one keeps the data's shape?
  4. Lagrange basis. Turn on the basis functions: each yⱼℓⱼ is zero at every node except its own. Their sum is the blue polynomial.
  5. Kinks and steps. Sample |x| or tanh(20x). Which interpolant converges fastest as n grows, and which is fooled?
