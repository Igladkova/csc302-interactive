Unit Summaries

CSc 302 · one page per meeting · CCNY

Interactive modules · CSc 302 Info · Calc pages

One page per CSc 302 meeting, in the style of the calculus review pages: learning objectives, the key results with Heath (and Stewart) section numbers, a figure, worked examples with hidden answers, a table of the mathematics the meeting needs (linked to the Calc pages, or for linear algebra to MATH 246), and review questions.

Heath §1.1–1.2 · Stewart §11.10–11.11 · meeting 1

Approximations; Taylor series with remainder

The strategy of scientific computing, sources of approximation, Taylor's theorem with remainder, choosing the degree, order of the error; what math it needs.

Heath §1.2 · meeting 2

Errors: absolute and relative, forward and backward; conditioning; stability

Significant digits, data vs computational error, truncation vs rounding, forward and backward error, condition numbers, an unstable recurrence; what math it needs.

Heath §1.3–1.4 · meeting 3

Floating-point systems, rounding, machine epsilon, cancellation

β, p, L, U; UFL, OFL and ε_mach; IEEE double; subnormals, Inf and NaN; non-associativity; cancellation and overflow and how to rewrite formulas; what math it needs.

Stewart §14.1–14.2 · meeting 4

Functions of several variables: graphs, level curves, limits

Surfaces, level curves and contour plots in Python, path tests and squeeze proofs for limits, continuity, a removable singularity in floating point; what math it needs.

Stewart §14.3 · Heath §8.6.1 · meeting 5

Partial derivatives; Clairaut; finite-difference partials

Partials as slopes of traces, higher derivatives and Clairaut, forward, centered and mixed differences, their errors and cost; what math it needs.

Stewart §14.4 · Heath §5.6.2 · meeting 6

Tangent planes, differentials, the Jacobian, multivariable Taylor

Linearization, error propagation by differentials, the Jacobian and F(x + s) ≈ F + Js, the quadratic model with gradient and Hessian; what math it needs.

Stewart §14.5–14.6 · meeting 7

The chain rule; directional derivatives and the gradient

Chain rule as a product of Jacobians, directional derivatives, the gradient as steepest ascent and normal to level sets, gradient checks; what math it needs.

Heath §2.1–2.3 · meeting 8

Linear systems: norms, condition number, error bounds

Existence and uniqueness, vector and matrix norms, cond(A), error bounds and digits lost, residual vs error, Hilbert matrices; what math it needs.

Heath §2.4 · meeting 9

Gaussian elimination, LU factorization, pivoting, operation counts

Triangular solves, elimination matrices and A = LU, why and how to pivot (PA = LU), n³/3 work, factor once and solve many; what math it needs.

Heath §2.5, 2.7 · meeting 10

Special systems: SPD and Cholesky, banded; software

Positive definiteness, Cholesky by hand and as a test, tridiagonal and banded solves in O(n), LAPACK/SciPy routines; what math it needs.

Heath §3.1–3.3 · meeting 11

Linear least squares: existence, normal equations, conditioning

Data fitting, normal equations, orthogonal projection and the pseudo-inverse, cond(A) and the angle θ, why AᵀA squares the condition number; what math it needs.

Heath §3.4–3.5, 3.8 · meeting 12

QR factorization: Householder, Givens, Gram–Schmidt; software

Least squares by QR, Householder reflections and Givens rotations by hand, classical vs modified Gram–Schmidt, rank deficiency, cost and accuracy; what math it needs.

Heath §4.3, 4.5.1–4.5.3 · meeting 13

Eigenvalues: conditioning; power, inverse and Rayleigh quotient iteration

Eigenvalue background, Bauer–Fike and sensitive nonsymmetric eigenvalues, power iteration, shifted inverse iteration, Rayleigh quotient iteration; what math it needs.

Heath §4.5.6, 4.7–4.8 · meeting 14

QR iteration; computing the SVD; software

QR iteration with shifts and deflation, Hessenberg reduction, the SVD and its uses, why not eig(AᵀA), NumPy/SciPy routines; what math it needs.

Heath §5.1–5.5.2 · meeting 17

Nonlinear equations: conditioning, convergence rates, bisection, fixed-point iteration

Existence and multiplicity, the condition number of a root, rates r and C, bisection step counts, fixed-point convergence; what math it needs.

Heath §5.5.3–5.5.7 · meeting 18

Newton's method, secant method, safeguarded methods

Newton's quadratic convergence and its failures, multiple roots, the secant rate 1.618, interpolation methods, Brent's hybrid; what math it needs.

Heath §5.6 · Stewart §14.4 · meeting 19

Systems of nonlinear equations: Newton and Broyden

Intersecting curves, conditioning via the Jacobian, fixed-point iteration, Newton as tangent planes, Broyden updates, damping; what math it needs.

Heath §6.1–6.3 · Stewart §14.7 · meeting 20

Extrema, the Hessian, convexity and optimality conditions

Local vs global minima, coercivity, convexity, first- and second-order conditions, Cholesky test, the √ε limit; what math it needs.

Heath §6.4 · meeting 21

One-dimensional optimization: golden section, parabolic interpolation, Newton

Unimodality, golden section and its step count, parabolic interpolation (r ≈ 1.324), Newton for minimization, Brent's safeguarded method; what math it needs.

Heath §6.5 · meeting 22

Unconstrained optimization: steepest descent, Newton, BFGS, conjugate gradients

Descent directions and line searches, zigzagging and the condition number, Newton steps, the BFGS update, conjugate directions; what math it needs.

Heath §6.2.3, 6.7 · Stewart §14.8 · meeting 23

Lagrange multipliers; constrained optimality; penalty methods and SQP

Lagrange conditions and their geometry, the multiplier as a sensitivity, the KKT matrix and inequality constraints, SQP as Newton's method, penalty methods; what math it needs.

Heath §7.1–7.3 · meeting 24

Polynomial interpolation: bases, Lagrange and Newton forms, Runge, Chebyshev

Existence and uniqueness, monomial vs Lagrange vs Newton, divided differences, Vandermonde conditioning, the error formula, Runge's phenomenon, Chebyshev points; what math it needs.

Heath §7.4 · Stewart §10.1, 13.1–13.2 · meeting 25

Piecewise and spline interpolation; parametric curves

Piecewise linear error, Hermite cubics and PCHIP, cubic splines and end conditions, overshoot, spline curves through points and their length; what math it needs.

Heath §8.1–8.3 · meeting 26

Numerical integration: Newton–Cotes, Gaussian, composite and adaptive quadrature

Conditioning of integration, midpoint/trapezoid/Simpson and their errors, degree, Gaussian rules, composite convergence rates, adaptive quadrature; what math it needs.

Heath §8.4.3–8.4.4 · Stewart §15.1, 15.3, 15.4, 15.9 · meeting 27

Double integrals, change of variables, product rules and Monte Carlo

Fubini, mass, center of mass and probability, polar coordinates and the Jacobian, tensor-product rules, the curse of dimensionality, Monte Carlo and σ/√N; what math it needs.

Heath §8.6–8.7 · meeting 28

Numerical differentiation, Richardson extrapolation, automatic differentiation; course review

Ill conditioning of differentiation, finite differences and the optimal h, Richardson and Romberg, forward-mode AD, and the course's error themes; what math it needs.

Textbooks: M. T. Heath, Scientific Computing: An Introductory Survey, revised 2nd ed., SIAM, 2018 · J. Stewart, D. Clegg and S. Watson, Calculus: Early Transcendentals, 9th ed., Cengage, 2021.
