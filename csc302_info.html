Catalog description

Approximation and error: Taylor series, floating-point arithmetic; conditioning and stability; error analysis.

Calculus of several variables for computation: functions of several variables; partial derivatives; tangent planes and linear approximation; the chain rule; gradients and directional derivatives; extrema and Lagrange multipliers; double integrals.

Numerical methods: linear systems and LU factorization, linear least squares and QR, eigenvalues and the SVD, nonlinear equations and systems, optimization, interpolation and splines, numerical integration and differentiation.

Pre-requisites: CSc 103, MATH 212, MATH 246

Co-requisite: CSc 303 Numerical Analysis Lab
