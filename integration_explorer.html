Try this

  1. Midpoint vs trapezoid. For e^(−x²), compare the two errors at any k: midpoint's is about half of trapezoid's, with the opposite sign (so Simpson = (2M + T)/3 cancels them).
  2. Order. Double k from 8 to 16. By what factor does each error shrink? Read off the order from the slopes in the chart.
  3. Exactness. Pick x³: Simpson and 2-point Gauss are exact already at k = 1. Why?
  4. Rough integrands. Pick √x or the step. Which rules lose their order? Where does adaptive Simpson put its intervals?
  5. Runge again. 1/(1 + 25x²) is easy to integrate piecewise, even though interpolating it by one polynomial failed in Chapter 7.
