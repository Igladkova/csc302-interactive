  1. Quadratic, κ = 5 (Heath 6.11–6.14), start (5, 1). Steepest descent zigzags; conjugate gradient finishes in 2 steps; Newton in 1. Raise κ to 100 and count steepest-descent steps.
  2. Learning rate. Turn off line search. For the quadratic, gradient descent converges only if α < 2/λmax = 2/κ. Set α just above and just below that value.
  3. Rosenbrock from (−1.2, 1). Which methods follow the banana valley, and how many gradient evaluations does each need?
  4. Himmelblau. Start near (−0.3, −0.9) with undamped Newton: it can head for the local maximum or a saddle. Turn damping on and repeat.
  5. Nelder–Mead. Shrink the expansion γ toward 1. What happens to the triangle on Rosenbrock?
  6. Bumpy bowl. Its Hessian is singular at the minimum. Why do Newton and BFGS lose their fast convergence here?
